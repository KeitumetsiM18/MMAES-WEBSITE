ABOUT MMAES

Building better decisions through better evidence.

Moloi Modern Assessment and Evaluation Solutions (MMAES) provides specialist training, consultancy and technical services in educational assessment, measurement and evaluation.

We work with institutions, projects and researchers to transform standards, data and research evidence into better educational decisions.

Our approach combines technical expertise with practical, decision-focused support across assessment, measurement, standard setting, data and evidence, monitoring and evaluation, training and reporting.
